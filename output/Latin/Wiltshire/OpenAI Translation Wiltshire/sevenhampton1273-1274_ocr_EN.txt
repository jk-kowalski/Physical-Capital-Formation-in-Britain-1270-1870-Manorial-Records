STRATTON: ACCOUNT OF JOHN DE CRUCE, REEVE OF STRATTON, UNDER THE TITLE ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ROLL ENTITLED

RECEIPTS (RECEPTUM)
The same renders account of 44 shillings 8 pence [xliiij s. viij d.] from the fixed rent per annum. From the customary aid this year, 20 shillings [xx s.]. From the toll of the fair of Saint Margaret, 29 shillings 11.5 pence [xxix s. xj d. ob.]. From Henry the carter for entry to land that was Ralph Basly’s, 22 shillings 4 pence [xxij s. iiij d.]. From William Reyburg for holding his mother’s land, 13 shillings 4 pence [xiiij s. iiij d.]. From the fine of Robert Sulman to be allowed to go wherever freely, 2 shillings [ij s.]. From winter pasture sold wholesale, 32 shillings 8 pence [xxxij s. viij d.]. From summer pasture of 54 oxen and 5 calves, 27 shillings 5 pence [xxvij s. v d.]. From herbage in the barton, 2 shillings 6 pence [ij s. vj d.]. From pasture in fallows, nothing, because sheep murrain was common in the country and no one wanted to take pasture. From the meadow of Wyslad this year, nothing, because it lies fallow. From hay sold wholesale this year, nothing, because it remains whole until the following year. From straw this year, nothing. From corn sold wholesale, 8 pounds [viij li.], and thus less this year because corn failed everywhere in the country.
TOTAL SUM OF RECEIPTS, 17 pounds 5 shillings 10.5 pence [xvij li. v s. x d. ob.].

EXPENSE
The same accounts in weeding the corn there, 6.5 pence [vj d. ob.]. In all the meadow there, mowing, spreading, and carrying by works, 10 shillings [x s.]. In beer, meat, fish, and cheese bought for autumn boon-workers reaping all the grain there, 6 shillings 8 pence [vj s. viij d.]. For a boon of 10 plowmen for barley, 10 pence [x d.].
TOTAL EXPENSES, 19.5 shillings [xviiij s. ob.]. And so he owes 16 pounds 7 shillings 10 pence [xvj li. vij s. x d.] which he delivered to the reeve of Sevenhampton. And so he is quit.
