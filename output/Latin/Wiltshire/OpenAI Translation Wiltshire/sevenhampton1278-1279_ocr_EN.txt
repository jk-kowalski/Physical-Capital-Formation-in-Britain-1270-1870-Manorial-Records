BAILIFF: ACCOUNT OF HENRY DE YAUNEWYCH, BAILIFF OF SEVENHAMPTON FOR THE YEAR WITHIN THIS ROLL AS TITLED.
RECEIPTS (RECEPTUM)
The same renders account of 52 pounds 17 shillings 9 pence [lij li. xvij s. ix d.] of arrears of the preceding account. And of 77 pounds 8 shillings [lxxvij li. viij s.] from John, reeve of Sevenhampton. And of 29 pounds [xxviiij li.] from Henry, reeve of Stratton. And of 9 pounds [ix li.] from John, reeve of Upton. And of 100 shillings [c s.] from Henry the chaplain of Somerford. And of 10 shillings [x s.] from Robert of Abingdon for scutage for a quarter part of a knight’s fee. And of 10 shillings [x s.] from Roger of Writele for the same. And of 10 shillings [x s.] from tenants of the Prior of Merton in Cheleworth for the same. And of 20 shillings [xx s.] from tenants of Grundewelle for half a knight’s fee. And of 40 shillings [xl s.] from Sir John Gulafre for one knight’s fee in Bluntesdon.
TOTAL OF ALL RECEIPTS 176 pounds 15 shillings 9 pence [clxxvj li. xv s. ix d.]
EXPENSES AND PAYMENTS (EXPENSE ET LIBERACIONES)
The same accounts in his fee per year, 100 shillings [c s.]. For 14 ells of blue cloth for the lord’s nephews, scholars at Oxford, bought, 30 shillings [xxx s.]. In money carried to the lord in London, 12 pence [xij d.]. For a fur purchase for the same Henry, 2 shillings 4 pence [ij s. iiij d.]. In payment made to the clerk of the hundreds of Worth and Cricklade, 10 shillings [x s.] for their wages. In payment made to William of Stratton, nephew of the lord, a scholar at Oxford, 26 pounds 12 shillings 8 pence [xxvj li. xij s. viij d.] by 6 letters patent. In payment made to the lord 46 pounds 14 shillings 2 pence [xlvj li. xiiijor s. ij d.] by one letter patent, and payment to the same lord, 9 pounds [ix li.] by the hands of William of Stratton, brother of the lord. In payment made to William of Stratton, brother of the lord, upon account, 9 pounds 1 mark [ix li. j m.]. In expenses for William of Stratton and John of Wydihull, auditing accounts of Sevenhampton, Stratton, and Upton, 17 shillings [xvij s.].
TOTAL EXPENSES AND PAYMENTS 100 pounds 6 pence [c li. vj d.]. And so there are owed to the lord 76 pounds 15 shillings 3 pence [lxxvj li. xv s. iij d.].
