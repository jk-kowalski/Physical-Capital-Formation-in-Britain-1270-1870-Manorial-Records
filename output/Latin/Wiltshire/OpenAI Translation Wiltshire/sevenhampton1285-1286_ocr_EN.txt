Of which, in acquittal of the reeve, 1 maker of ploughs, 6 ploughmen, 1 cowman and 1 shepherd, 80 works [de iiijxx opera]. In allowance for the reeve for 2 feast days falling on working days in autumn this year, 72 works [lxxij opera]. In weeding 348 acres of all kinds of grain, 324 works [cccxxiiij opera]. In 48.5 acres of meadow mowing, lifting, and making cock hay, 567 works [et dimidia prati falcandis levandis et mullonibus in prato faciendis dlxvij opera]. In 18 acres of rewayn meadow mowing, lifting, and carting, 80 works [xviij acris prati de rewayn falcandis levandis et cariandis iiijxx opera]. In the same hay carting and making cocks in Berton, 116 works [cxvj opera]. In 332 acres of all kinds of grain reaping and binding, 648 works [cccxxxij acris ... metendis et ligandis dcxlviiij opera]. In allowance for the reeve for 1 boon of 44 men who harvested for a whole day 16 acres, 44 works [xliiij opera]. In bringing in and stacking the said grain in the grange, 144 works [cxliiij opera]. In making 1 cock of forage in Berton, 44 works [j mullone foragii in bertona faciendo xliiij opera]. In straw spreading and gathering by pieces, 234 works [stipula prosternenda et colligenda per particulas ccxxxiiij opera].
NEDBENE. The same renders account of 54 works from the source called nedbene [liiiij operibus de exitu qui vocatur nedbene]. Of which, in acquitting the reeve and 9 men as above, 10 works [x opera]. In 16 acres of all kinds of grain reaping and binding, 44 works [xvj acris omnimodi bladi metendis et ligandis xliiij opera]. And so equal.
BAILIFF: ACCOUNT OF HENRY DE AUNEWYK, BAILIFF OF SEVENHAMPTON FOR THE YEAR WITHIN THIS ROLL TITLED.
RECEIPTS (RECEPTUM)
The same renders account of 62 pounds 10 shillings [lxij li. x s.] received from John the reeve of Stratton. And of 24 pounds 2 shillings 0.25 pence [xxiiij li. ij s. q.] from William the reeve of Upton. And of 24 marks [xxiiij m.] from Walter the chaplain of Denchesworth. And of 8 marks [viij m.] from Henry the chaplain of Somerford. And of 208 pounds 10 shillings [ccviij li. x s.] from Thomas the reeve of Sevenhampton.
TOTAL RECEIPTS 316 pounds 8 shillings 2.25 pence [cccxvj li. viij s. viij d. q.]
EXPENSES (EXPENSE)
The same accounts for his fee per year, 10 marks [x m.]. For the expenses of William of Stratton and John of Wydihull from London to Sevenhampton and staying there for 10 days to hear the accounts of Sevenhampton, Stratton, and Upton of this year, and in their

expenses in returning towards London, 34 shillings [xxxiiij s.]. For parchment, 12 pence [xij d.]. For fur bought for the use of the same Henry by the order of the lord, 2 shillings 6 pence [ij s. vj d.]. For silver taken to London one time, 2 shillings [ij s.].
TOTAL 8 pounds 12 shillings 10 pence [viij li. xij s. x d.]
PAYMENTS OF MONEY (LIBERATIONES DENARIORUM)
The same accounts in payment made to the lord, 16 pounds 1 mark [xvj li. j m.] by one letter patent. Also, in payment made to William of Stratton on account, 204 pounds 2 shillings 6.25 pence [cc iiijxx li. ij s. vj d. q.]
TOTAL 307 pounds 15 shillings 10.25 pence [CCCVij li. xv s. x d. q.]
GRAND TOTAL OF EXPENSES AND PAYMENTS 316 pounds 8 shillings 2.25 pence [CCCXVj li. viij s. viij d. q.]. And so equal.
