Walter Crume holds one messuage and half a virgate of land and pays annually at the Nativity of the Lord 7.5 pence [vij d. ob.] and at Easter 7.5 pence [vij d. ob.] and at the feast of the Nativity of St John the Baptist 7.5 pence [vij d. ob.] and at the feast of St Michael for medgavel 19 pence [xviiij d.] and at the feast of St Martin in winter one cock valued at 1 penny [j d.] and 4 hens. Value of a hen 1.5 pence [j d. ob.]. And he shall plough and harrow for wheat sowing for one day and for fallow for one day according to the number of his beasts for the plough. Value of a day’s food 3 pence [iij d.]. And he shall wash and shear sheep for two days. Value of a day’s food 0.25 pence [q.]. And he shall wash and shear lambs for one day. Value of the day’s food 0.25 pence [q.]. And he shall pay for each beast so it can pasture in the countess’s pasture at the feast of St Thomas the Apostle 0.25 pence [q.]. And he shall work from the feast of St John the Baptist until Lammas day each week with one man for 3 days without food and drink up to the ninth hour. Value of each work 1 penny [j d.]. And from Lammas to the feast of St Michael each week for 5 days with one man until the ninth hour. Value of the day 1 penny [j d.]. And he shall find 2 men for every boonwork in autumn. Value of each day’s food 3 pence [iij d.]. And he shall give pannage if he has pigs, that is, for a pig 1 penny [j d.] and for a piglet 0.5 pence [ob.]. And if he has sheep they shall be in the countess’s fold from Hokeday until the feast of St Martin. And he shall tread with bare feet on the threshing floor in the court for half a day, value 0.25 pence [q.].

Matilda Slykelyng holds half a messuage and a quarter of a virgate of land and pays annually at the above terms 11.25 pence [xj d. q.] and at the feast of St Martin half a cock and 2 hens. Value as above. And she shall do half the services of Walter above.
RENT OF THE COTTARS OF SEVENHAMTON
David the shoemaker holds one messuage and one croft which contains one acre of land and pays annually at the above terms 9 pence [ix d.]. And he shall work from the feast of St John the Baptist until the feast of St Michael each week on Monday. Value of a day’s work 1 penny [j d.]. And he shall find 2 men for the first boonwork of the countess. Value of a day’s work 4 pence [iiij d.]. And if other boonworks occur on a Monday, he shall find 2 men. Value of a day’s work as above. And if it occurs on another day, he shall find 1 man. Value of a day’s work 1.5 pence [j d. ob.]. And he shall wash sheep for one day and shear for 1 day. Value 0.25 pence [q.]. And he shall make the sheepfold in the court whenever necessary.
Elias Fot holds one messuage and one croft which contains one acre of land and pays annually at the above terms 9 pence [ix d.]. And he shall do all services as above.
