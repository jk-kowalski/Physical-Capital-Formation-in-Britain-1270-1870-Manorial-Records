Dairy (daeria).
The same answers for 35 cheeses of rewann which began to be made on the morrow of Saint Michael and ceased to be made on the vigil of blessed Catherine, namely for two days 1 cheese each and afterwards 6 each day in turn. Total 35. And in overselling 35 and nothing remains.

The same answers for 202 cheeses that began to be made 11 days before the Kalends of May and ceased at the feast of St Michael, that is, from the aforesaid term they made 1 cheese per day until 11 days before the Kalends of June, and then they began to make 2 cheeses per day until the first day of July, and then made 1 cheese per day until the feast of Saint Michael. Total 202. Of these, 14 for tithe [decima xiiij]. 128 were oversold which made 8 cheeses [supravendicione c iiij*viij qui fecerunt viij pisas]. Thus 202. Finished.
