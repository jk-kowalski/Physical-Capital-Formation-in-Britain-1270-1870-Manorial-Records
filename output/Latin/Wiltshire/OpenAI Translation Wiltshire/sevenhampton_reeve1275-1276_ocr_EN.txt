Livestock (Instaurrum)
For horses.
The same answers for 1 gelding and 5 brood mares and for 1 female foal aged 3.5 years [etate iij annorum et dimidii] and for 1 foal aged 1.5 years and for 12 mares received from Navesby. Total 20. From these sent to Wytchur’, 1 gelding and 8 mares. In death, 1 whose hide was tanned. In release by the countess’s license, 1 mare. In sale, 3 mares. Total 14, and there remain 5 mares and 1 foal aged 2.5 years [ij annorum et dimidii]. The same answers for 3 foals of last year’s birth [anni preteriti] in reserve. Total 4. Of these, 1 died and remain 3 foals aged 1.5 years [j anni et dimidii], one of them a female. The same answers for 1 male foal from the yield and it remains.
For oxen.
The same answers for 62 oxen in reserve and for 3 oxen bought and for 30 oxen received from Navesby and for 6 added. Total 101 oxen. From these, sold 24, of whom 1 was dead and 1 was sick. Sent to the countess at Tottehal’, 12. Total 36 and there remain 66 oxen.

From the proceeds of the dairy.
The same answers for 46 cheeses of rennet made, which began to be made on the morrow of Saint Michael, one cheese each day up to and including the following Sunday, except Thursday, and from then they began to make 1 cheese every 2 days and none on the third day up to the morrow of Saint Frideswide, and from then they made 1 cheese every other day up to the Conception of Blessed Mary, and then made 6 cheeses and rested. Of these, in the expenses of the accountants, 1 cheese. In sale, 29, which made 1 cheese wheel, in sale by hand without the wheel, 16. Total 46 cheeses and none remain.
In summer.
The same answers for 229 cheeses made, beginning on the Sunday called “clausum Pasche”, namely the second Ides of April, and ending on the vigil of Saint Michael, both days included, for on the day of Saint Michael, none were made; that is, from the said Sunday they made one cheese per day up to the Sunday before Pentecost, that is, the 16th day before the Kalends of June, and in that time they made 7 small cheeses at the price of 1 penny [j d.], and on the said Sunday they began to make two cheeses per day up to the morrow of Saint Thomas the Martyr, and on that day began to make one because of drought until the vigil of Saint Michael. Total 229. Of these, in tithe, 16 and 1 small. In expenses of Thomas the clerk, 1 small cheese. In sale to the same Thomas, 5 small cheeses. In sales over all, 116 which made 11 cheese wheels and half and a quarter of a wheel. Total 229.
