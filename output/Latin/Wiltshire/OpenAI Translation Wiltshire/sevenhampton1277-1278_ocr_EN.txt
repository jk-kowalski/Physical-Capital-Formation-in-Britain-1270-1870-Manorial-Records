And thus the aforesaid Henry still owes the lord 53 pounds 19 shillings 11.25 pence [liij li. xix s. xj d. q.]. Of which he paid upon account in the expenses of the accountants 22 shillings 2.25 pence [xxij s. ij d. q.]. And so the said Henry still owes the lord in clear amount 52 pounds 17 shillings 9 pence [lij li. xvij s. ix d.].
