total 34 pounds 2 shillings 4.75 pence [xxxiiij li. ij s. iiij d. ob. q.]
Of which, for weeding the grain, 7 pence [vij d.]. For meadows this year being mowed, gathered, and stacked in ricks by works, in company, 5 shillings 0.5 pence [v s. ob.]. For ale, meat, cheese, and fish for the boonworks and workers reaping grain in autumn, 2 shillings 5.5 pence [ij s. v d. ob.]. For the expenses of keeping the fair of St Margaret and collecting tolls, 3 shillings [iij s.]. And let it be known that in previous years they took no allocation because they expended for the profit of those same fairs and did not count or charge them in their receipts.
total 12 shillings [xij s.]. And delivered to the reeve of Sevenhampton, 33 pounds 10 shillings 4.75 pence [xxxiij li. x s. iiij d. ob. q.]. And so he is quit.
WHEAT (FRUMENTUM).
From the yield, nothing because it is sold in bulk. Received from the reeve of Sevenhampton, 5.5 quarters and a half bushel [v quarteria ij busselli et dimidius]. Of which, for seed on 20 acres, 4.25 quarters and a half bushel [iiij quarteria j bussellus et dimidius]. In expenses about mowing meadows and harvesting and gathering grain, 1.25 quarters 1 bushel [j quarterium j bussellus]. Total as before. And likewise.

BARLEY (ORDEUM).
From the yield, nothing this year. Received from the reeve of Sevenhampton, 6.75 quarters 3 bushels [vj quarteria iij busselli]. Of which, for seed on 19.5 acres, 5.5 quarters 2 bushels [v quarteria dimidium ij busselli]. And 5 bushels are lacking, which are sold in the account.
DREDGE (DRAG’).
The same renders account for 1.25 quarters 3 bushels of dredge received from Sevenhampton [j quarterio iij bussellis]. And sown on 4.5 acres [iiij acras et dimidiam].
OATS (AVENA).
The same renders account for no yield of oats this year. Received from the reeve of Sevenhampton, 2.75 quarters 7 bushels [ij quarteria vij busselli]. And sown on 8 acres [viij acras].
