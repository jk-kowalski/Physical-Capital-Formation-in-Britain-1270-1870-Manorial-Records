Capons
The same renders account for 18 capons from produce. — Of which sent to W. de Bereford, 6. And in expenses for two Friars Minor, 1 capon. And in death, 2 capons. — and <...> remainder, 17.
Hens
The same renders account for <...> 38 hens from produce. And 30 from output. And 21 from the rent of the villeins at the feast of St. Martin. Total <...> 87 [lxxxvii].
Of which sent to Oxford, 30 hens by tally. And to the lords upon account, 8 hens and 2 cocks. And in the aforesaid upon account, 18 chicks from output. — Total 68. — and remainder, 29 between large and small <...> and remainder of small chicks — 6.
Doves (Columbe)
The same renders account for 202 dovecote pigeons from output from the feast of St. Michael to the feast of St. James. — Total as appears.
Of which in tithe, 20 pigeons. And in despatch to Oxford, 110 pigeons by 4 tallies. And in sale, 72.
Dairy (Daeria)
The same renders account for 56 cheeses from output from the Friday after the Chains of St. Peter up to the feast of St. Michael. And 9 beyond the feast of St. Michael.
Total 65.
Of which in tithe, 7 cheeses. And in the above sale, 58 cheeses. — Total 65. — and remainder, nothing.
The same renders account for 80 cheeses from the produce of this year, which began to be made on the morrow of St. Mark and continued in one form up to Ascension Day, and were made every second day. And from that day they were made every day and kept in another form up to Trinity Day, and from that day in the form now used. This output is from the morrow of St. Mark up to St. James’s Day.

[Page 13]
Of which in tithe, 8 cheeses. And in arrival of the warden, 1 cheese by tally of expenses. And in sale, 12 cheeses. And in lord’s expenses upon account, 10 cheeses. And on 3 days there was a deficiency due to milk being used in the kitchen and for the Lords’ Supper upon account. — Total 34. — and remainder, 46.

Also renders account for 1 new cart iron-bound with harness, and 1 cart not bound with a saddle and old iron for an old cart to be bound.
Also renders account for 1 bronze pot of 4 gallons and 1 bronze pot of 1.5 gallons and 1 trivet and 1 pan and 1 grill.
Also renders account for 1 haircloth and 1 lead.
