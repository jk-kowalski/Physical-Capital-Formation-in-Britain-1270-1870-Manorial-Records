Account of Ricardus le Blunt, reeve of Cuxham, from the feast of Saint Agatha the Virgin in the fourth year of the reign of King Edward, up to the feast of Saint Michael next following
"Memorandum concerning the arrears of the last account, 27 shillings [xxvij s.]. Total 27 shillings [xxvij s.]."
