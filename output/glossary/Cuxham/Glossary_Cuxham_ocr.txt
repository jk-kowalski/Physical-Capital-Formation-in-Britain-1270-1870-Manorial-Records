2. A glossarial  index  to all words  or spellings  that  are not included  in either  C. T. Lewis

(i) words  that form  part  of the name  of a person  or place;  (ii) words  or spellings  that  are
probably  clerical  errors  and are noted  (with  or without  question-mark)  in the footnotes  to
the text;  (iii) words  made  up of a preposition  and a noun  written  together  (e.g.  ‘inmorina’)

Latham,  op. cit., even  though  they  are recorded  there  only  from  a different  period  or from
glossaries.
The  case,  number,  etc.,  of cited  words  are given  only  where  these  are known  from  the
external  evidence  of the context,  not just  from  the form  of the word  itself.  Where  a word  is
cited  in italics  it is understood  to cover  all inflexions.  References  are to the numbers  of the
documents;  in the case  of accounts  a note  of the relevant  paragraph  is added  in parentheses.

Declension.B. GRAMMAR  AND  SYNTAX
For correct  use of fem. for names  of trees  see fagus,  fraxinus.  ‘eundem’  is
used  to refer  to the English  cogwe/  on 73 (costs  of mill).
For errors  of gender  see haia,  petra,  sepes,  transgressio  and,  possibly,
corda;  see also boviculus  for use of masc.  noun  referring  to female  stock.
Hand  xxi writes  ‘que dati fuerunt’  on 67, 68 (oats  in each)  and hand  xxiii
twice  writes  *quilibet  of mulieres,  77 (hoeing  and mowing);  ‘Matilde...
defuncti  occurs  on 94, ‘pro dampna  facto’  on 99, ‘inparcata’  referring  to
auce  on 108.
Where  there  is only  a single  item  of stock  in a paragraph  on an account
roll the heading  is extended  in the sing.  (MS.  : ‘Taurus’  76). ‘Apri’,  however,
occurs  in such  a case on 64, ‘Hogastri’  on 67, ‘Sues’  on 71 and ‘Iercie’  on
72. Where  an animal  is added  ‘cum...’  or ‘de...  .' the noun  is extended
throughout  in the pl.
For the cheese  paragraphs  in hand  xii the items  accounted  for in account-
roll entries  are extended  in acc. (MS.:  *quolibet  die vnum  cas’’  65 (cheese);
etc.),  but otherwise  in nom.  throughout  (MSS.:  ‘baco  de stauro’  67
(visitors’  exp.);  ‘j capo’  70 (visitors’  exp.);  ‘j cecus'  84 (carthorses);  etc.).
‘per noctem  j garbam’  occurs  on 68 (oats).
Gen.  used  for age occurs  in ‘dimidii  anni’  58 (sale  of stock);  see also vetus.
Noun  following  copulative  verb extended  throughout  to same  case as the
subject  (MS.:  ‘vnus  factus  est equs’  62 (foals)).  ‘de x Hogerellis  rem’
super  comp’  angni’  occurs  on 67 (hogerelli).
Errors  of case mostly  arise  from  a tendency  to lapse  into the nominative,
e.g. ‘cuidam  mercatori  de lond’  iam defunctus’  61 (gifts),  ‘In j pertica  et
ij pedes  noui  muri’  65 (petty  exp.).
3rd decl. adjectives,  including  present  participles,  extended  in abl. sing.
to -e or -i following  the usage  of Classical  Latin  (MSS.:  *precedenti'  97;
‘in separali  solo’ 107; etc.). More  often,  however,  the MSS.  use -e in all
circumstances  for abl. sing. of present  participles  (‘faciente’  35 (petty  exp.);
‘currente’  107; etc.). ‘separali’  ab/. s., though  used as a noun,  occurs  on 127.
For hands  xvii, xxiii,  comparative  adjectives  extended  to -i in abl. singular,
otherwise  to -e (MSS.:  ‘maiori’  64 (sale of dairy  produce),  69 (cheese);
*minore'  80 (sale of dairy  produce);  etc.). ‘priori’  occurs  on 77 (note  after
cash balance:  hand xxv), ‘maiori’  on 80 (cheese),  'meliori'  on 97.
